6. AR MODEL SUMMARY

The SARIMA Equation

SARIMA (1,1,1)x(1,1,1)12

(1 - \phi_1 L)(1 - L^{12})Y_t = (1 + \theta_1 L)(1 + \Theta_1 L^{12})\epsilon_t

Interpretation of Coefficients

  • AR(1) $\phi$: Momentum (Autoregressive term).
  • MA(1) $\theta$: Error Correction (Moving Average term).
  • Seasonal L12: Annual Seasonality Component.
SEASONAL SUBSERIES: Month-wise mean deviations.
